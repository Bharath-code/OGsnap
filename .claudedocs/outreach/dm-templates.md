# DM templates

Send at most 10 a day. Fill every `{}` from the audit. Never send a generic version; if you can't fill a field truthfully, skip the prospect.
Attach the site's own before/after image (inner page, not homepage).

## A: Per-page gap (default)
> Hi {first name}, I shared {inner page URL} and the preview shows {homepage title} with the homepage image, so every page on {site} looks the same when it's posted. I built a tool that gives each page its own branded preview, automatically, no design work. Attached is what {inner page} would look like. Want me to set it up on {site} for you? Free for the first week.

## B: Truly missing preview
> Hi {first name}, I pasted {site} into LinkedIn and X and no preview image shows up, so it's just a bare link. I made one for you (attached). If you want it live on the site I can do the install myself. Happy to?

## C: Agency / freelancer
> Hi {name}, you build sites for clients on {platform}. Do their pages show the right preview when shared? I made a tool that sets a branded preview per page for every client site. $49 a month for 10 sites. Can I run it on one of your client sites for free so you can see?

## Rules
- One question per message. No links besides their own site and ours.
- No reply after 4 days: one follow-up, then stop.
- Log a reason for every "no" (one line). That is the data.
