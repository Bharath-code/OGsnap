Reading the threat

Lovable stops one step short, and that step is your opening

Lovable's agent can already fix titles and tags. A generated per-route image isn't documented, though a chat request could plausibly produce a Satori route on the new stack.J Assume the single-platform window could close in any release.

A cross-platform promise holds up longer: an agency with Lovable, Framer and Shopify clients wants one report covering all of them. No platform has a reason to verify its rivals' sites.

Strengths

Weaknesses

  • No customers, no deploy, no conversationsMSend 10 DMs before finishing deployment.
